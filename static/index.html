About IronPeak

Built for people who show up.

Founded in 2014 by a crew of strength coaches and ex-athletes, IronPeak is a 32,000 sq ft training facility designed around one belief: real results come from great coaching, great equipment and a community that pushes you forward.

From your very first assessment to your hundredth PR, our coaches build a plan around you, not a template.

  • ⬢
    Competition-grade equipment

    Eleiko bars, calibrated plates, 14 power racks and a full turf lane.

  • ⬢
    Certified coaching team

    Every coach holds NSCA or NASM credentials and keeps learning.

  • ⬢
    Recovery zone

    Infrared sauna, cold plunge and mobility tools to keep you training.
