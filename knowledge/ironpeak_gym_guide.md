### Billing and membership policies
- **Joining / enrolment fee:** none.
- **Contract:** no long-term contract on any plan. Monthly plans renew month to month.
- **Annual plans:** paid once per year upfront, which works out to 2 months free compared with paying monthly.
- **Cancelling:** you can cancel anytime from the IronPeak app or at the front desk. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.
- **Pausing (freezing):** you can pause your membership anytime, for example for travel or injury, from the app or the front desk.
- **Switching plans:** you can upgrade or downgrade between Base Camp, Summit and Apex Elite at any time. The new price applies from the next billing period.
- **Unused perks:** guest passes and personal training sessions are monthly allowances.
- **Payment methods:** major credit and debit cards, paid through the IronPeak app or at the front desk.

## 5. Training Programs

1. **Strength Training** (all levels): progressive barbell and dumbbell programming that builds real-world strength and muscle.
2. **HIIT & Conditioning** (high intensity): heart-pounding intervals with ropes, sleds and bikes that burn calories and build fitness.
3. **Group Classes** (38 per week): high-energy coached classes kept small enough that every rep gets coached.
4. **Yoga & Mobility** (recovery): restores range of motion, reduces injury risk and builds the mobility lifting depends on.
5. **Personal Training** (1-on-1): one-on-one coaching with a custom program, form feedback and accountability. Included in Apex Elite (4 sessions/month). Members on other plans can ask about personal training through the contact form or front desk.
6. **Core & Pilates** (low impact): precision core work for a stronger midline, better posture and a healthier back.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

**Is there a joining fee?** No. There is no joining or enrolment fee on any plan.

**Do I have to sign a contract?** No. All plans are contract-free and can be cancelled or paused anytime.

**Which plan is the cheapest?** Base Camp at $39/month, or $390/year ($32.50/month).

**Which plan is most popular?** Summit, at $69/month or $690/year.

**Which plan includes personal training?** Apex Elite includes 4 personal training sessions per month.

**Are group classes included?** Yes, unlimited group classes are included in Summit and Apex Elite. They are not included in Base Camp.

**Can I bring a friend?** Summit and Apex Elite members get 2 guest passes per month.

**Do you have a sauna?** Yes. The recovery zone has an infrared sauna and a cold plunge, available on Summit and Apex Elite.

**Is the gym open 24/7?** Yes, for members with keycard access. Staffed hours are Mon–Fri 5 AM–10 PM and Sat–Sun 7 AM–8 PM.

**Is there a free trial?** Yes: 7 days free with unlimited access, classes and one coaching session. No card required.

**I'm a beginner. Is IronPeak for me?** Yes. Every new member gets a free fitness assessment, coaches build a plan around you, and there are beginner-friendly classes (see section 6).

**How do I sign up or ask something not covered here?** Use the contact form on the website, call (303) 555-0142, email hello@ironpeakfitness.com or visit 1450 Larimer Street, Denver.
